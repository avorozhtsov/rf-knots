## Completion, budget and stop/go

An exact braid-state join at reference index >=2 is a *candidate* only. Replay the full generated prefix plus the reference suffix to the unknot in the original ActionSpec; require original caps, no repeated prefix state, legal first action **and a first successor state different from the reference**, and strict L10 or L1000 cost difference versus the replayed constructive reference. Shared-prefix-only divergences, same-successor aliases, immediate undo loops, cap-exhausted searches and unverified joins yield no F pair. Save every search episode and independent auditor recomputes each bridge, SHA, start, cap, CC/moves/cost, CPU and neural work. Only the exact TRAIN root with used_cc=used_moves=0 could later get F gradient; intermediate heldout-owned states never become rows/negatives. No bank or trainer is mutated by this pilot.

Per-condition CPU ceiling **180 seconds**, total saved-work ceiling **1800 CPU seconds**; a timed-out condition is censored, and the collector stops between conditions when the total ceiling is reached. A single condition may overshoot the total ceiling by at most its per-condition limit. Run a first shard with `--stop-after 1`, independently audit its SHA/full replay/bridge counts, then `--resume` only if healthy and pins match. If all 16 conditions complete with 0 distinct strict-cost root forks, stop this source; with exactly1, replicate on a separately frozen panel before scaling; with >=2, a separately frozen small scale-up may be considered only after full independent audit and measured CPU per root. If the CPU ceiling stops before16, report the censored partial yield and do **not** declare a win or scale automatically. Four roots cannot justify a 30k campaign.

No TEST150/viewed DEV selection, no claimed superiority without a separately preregistered fully non-neural matched control, no unknotdb mutation/publication, cloud purchase, commit, merge or push. Preserve dirty files, failures and checkpoints.

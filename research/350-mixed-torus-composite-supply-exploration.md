# Exploratory mixed-torus composite source check (not F data)

2026-10-13. After the audited tie in `research/349`, run a **read-only, unpinned exploratory supply check** of a source different from the conjugate-witness and short-positive interleaved families: braid connected sums `T(2,2m+1) # mirror(T(2,2n+1))` for `m,n=1..12`. Each three-strand root is `sigma_1^(2m+1) sigma_2^-(2n+1)`. Apply `m` crossing-change/reduce pairs to the first block, `n` pairs to the second, then two destabilizations. The program was full-replayed for every otherwise-novel TRAIN candidate. This is the standard connected-sum construction and may be algorithmically easy to decompose; that is a scientific limitation, not evidence of learned search.

Of 144 ordered pairs, root ownership was TRAIN90, validation34, test19, quarantine1. After excluding legacy groups and the four prior TRAIN rosters plus nine viewed DEV panels, 50 ordered TRAIN pairs in 25 distinct *new candidate query groups* remained. All 50 reference programs replayed, fit paired L10/L1000 witness caps, and had no exact root-state overlap with the viewed panel witness trajectories. Symmetric `(m,n)` and `(n,m)` frequently share a group, hence pair count is not group count. Of the 25 groups, only one has constructive CC <=6; the other 24 require CC >=9. No alternate completed path, strict cost diversity, root divergence, MCTS-derived F coefficient, or independent frozen audit was produced. Therefore **zero new F-usable roots** may be claimed or added to the immutable 402-root bank.

Decision: this elementary mixed-torus grid is not a credible near-term small-MCTS yield source, and 25 groups are far from the 1k/3k/10k/30k target. Do not bulk-scale or train on it. Next read-only source audit should inventory genuinely different, replay-verifiable root/path supply already present in local evidence corpora (including UnknotDB exports where authorized), deduplicate by TRAIN query group and root exact state, and quantify full-path, root-divergent *strict-cost* pairs before any prospectively frozen MCTS pilot. Do not mutate/publish UnknotDB, reuse viewed DEV/TEST for selection, or count candidates as F labels. No cloud purchase, commit, merge or push.

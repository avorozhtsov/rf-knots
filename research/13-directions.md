# Current research directions

This page is the short active map. Detailed historical attempts were moved to
[`../archive/pre-semantic-moves-v1/`](../archive/pre-semantic-moves-v1/) when the
objective was corrected to count semantic moves only.

## 1. A reproducible scientist roster

Train four stable architecture families from scratch under the same objective
conditioning, remaining-budget input, auxiliary heads, replay contract, and
source-disjoint curriculum. Search compute is run metadata and is not embedded in
architecture names. See [16](16-scientists-collaboration.md).

## 2. Collaboration as a controlled data intervention

Compare adaptive/static scheduling and sharing/no-sharing using paired seeds and
matched compute. A donation is eligible only when its replayed semantic objective
strictly beats the receiver's incumbent. Evaluate the final solved sets, their
differences, and capped objective—not only route loss or aggregate solve count.

## 3. Scalable representations

Develop strand-count-agnostic raster and cyclic-memory scientists whose local
blocks share weights. The main roster retains `raster-axial`. The full-raster
`raster-routed` candidate learned feasibility but failed early objective-quality
and efficiency gates, so scalable global routing remains a separate development
direction rather than an assumption. See [17](17-scalable-braid-raster.md) and
[18](18-raster-representation.md).

## 4. Better unknotting-number upper bounds

After the collaboration protocol passes a 100+ representation gate, apply the
best admitted portfolio to fixed hard-knot targets and publish every improved
upper bound with replayable witnesses and source provenance. See
[19](19-superseding-the-rl-unknotter.md).

## 5. Capacity growth only after a measured capacity limit

Use function-preserving widening or block stacking only after increased search no
longer explains the deficit and a larger model wins a compute-matched gate. Keep
the old checkpoint as a teacher for migration verification, not as an undisclosed
starting advantage. See [06](06-network-growth.md).

The fixed window/scan/tape recombination was an interesting attempt, not a current
direction. Its full record is archived at
[`15-recombination-triad-attempt.md`](../archive/pre-semantic-moves-v1/research/15-recombination-triad-attempt.md).

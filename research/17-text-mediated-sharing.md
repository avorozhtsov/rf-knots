# Text-mediated sharing

Verified witness sharing teaches a route for one representation. A later research
direction is to let scientists propose reusable concepts, invariants, or tactics
and let other scientists test them.

This is scientifically distinct from the first collaboration experiment. It needs
four independently gated capabilities:

1. a writer extracts a compact claim from multiple verified witnesses;
2. a reader conditions ordinary search on the claim;
3. the claim predicts outcomes on source-disjoint representations; and
4. counterfactual removal of the claim removes the measured gain.

A useful invented invariant must be executable or falsifiable: it maps a braid
state to a stable value, predicts a bounded property, and survives known
equivalences. Natural-language plausibility is not evidence.

Do not include this arm in the initial five-arm witness-sharing experiment. Open it
only after ordinary sharing, no-sharing, and scheduling effects are understood.
